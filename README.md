## 📖 What Is This Mod?

SimpleBackpack gives you portable storage you can carry, wear, and upgrade — so you don't have to run back to your chests as often. Wear your gear in dedicated equipment slots instead of eating up your regular inventory space.

## 🎒 What You Get

| Item | Storage | What It's For |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiver** | 18 slots | Holds your arrows and automatically feeds your bow or crossbow when you run out in your main inventory |
| **Pouch** | 18 slots | Quick-access storage for potions, food, and other small stackables |
| **Backpack** | 27 slots | General-purpose storage you can wear |
| **Big Backpack** | 54 slots | An upgraded Backpack with double the space |
| **Ender Backpack** | 27 slots | Wearable, portable access to your personal Ender Chest storage |

You also get **6 dedicated equipment slots** (1 for your Quiver, 5 for Backpack-tier items) shown right in your inventory screen, so wearing these items doesn't cost you a single regular inventory slot.

## 🍳 Crafting

- **Backpack**: Wrap a Shulker Box in leather, wool, honeycomb, and string. Any color Shulker Box works, and its contents carry over — nothing is lost.
- **Big Backpack**: Combine two Backpacks. Their contents merge together into the bigger storage.
